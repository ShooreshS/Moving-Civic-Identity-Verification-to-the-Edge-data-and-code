The SHA-256 values identify retained private source files. Without those files, reviewers cannot validate their contents, reproduce the reported analyses, or establish that the summaries were derived correctly. Package integrity checks concern the files actually distributed here. See [measurement methods](measurement-methods.md) for the measurement boundaries and optional checks.

Pseudocode can support review of the stated implementation logic. It cannot replace missing empirical evidence or establish security properties by description. Broader manuscript claims need separate evidence or a narrower scope.
